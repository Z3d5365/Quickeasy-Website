Estimating & quoting

Increase Margins with Faster, Accurate Estimating

QuickEasy BOS Professional — the leading ERP system for printing, signage, and packaging brands — generates incredibly accurate estimates and quotes. It only takes a few clicks to take wastage, ink consumption, machine speed and shop floor overheads into account.

It even shows you how to lay out a job to reduce wastage and calculates the exact cost of unavoidable wastage to the penny — so even inexperienced estimators can create quotes, quickly and accurately, no matter how bespoke the job is.
