Cloud-Based ERP

If you already know your business needs ERP, there is another important decision to make. Where do you want that ERP to live? You can maintain the server environment yourself, take responsibility for backups, hardware and the technical infrastructure behind it, or you can opt for the ease and security of cloud-based ERP.

QuickEasy BOS gives you a complete cloud-based ERP without requiring you to run the core ERP server environment from your own premises. You still get the depth of QuickEasy ERP for SMEs across finance, distribution, manufacturing, sales and wider business operations. The difference is that QuickEasy can host the ERP environment for you, giving authorised users remote access without your business having to maintain the main BOS server on site. QuickEasy also supports locally hosted deployments where that is the better fit.

For many growing operational businesses, this is why QuickEasy BOS is such a strong cloud-based ERP choice. You get the power of a complete ERP, while reducing how much of the technology underneath it you have to worry about yourself.

Ready for the best cloud-based ERP? Let’s talk.

Key takeaways

  • QuickEasy BOS is a complete cloud-based ERP for businesses that want their core ERP environment hosted rather than maintained entirely on their own premises.
  • QuickEasy supports both cloud and local hosting.
  • For dispersed teams, or growing teams, cloud-based ERP systems are ideal.
  • Cloud-hosted QuickEasy can make ERP access easier for businesses operating across offices, factories, warehouses and other locations.
  • For QuickEasy-managed AWS hosting, we offer daily backups, securities, and recovery provisions.
  • Cloud hosting is not automatically right for every company. Connectivity, IT requirements, control and business continuity should all form part of the decision.

What is cloud-based ERP?

Cloud-based ERP is simply business management software that runs online, rather than being installed and hosted on a server at your own premises. With QuickEasy BOS cloud-based ERP, your business gets access to QuickEasy’s wider ERP system, while the main BOS system is hosted securely in the cloud.

For you as a business owner, the biggest difference with cloud-based ERP is who has to look after the technology behind the system.

If you host your ERP on your own server, your business is responsible for that server. You need to maintain it, keep it secure, back it up, deal with problems, and eventually replace it.

With QuickEasy BOS in the cloud, much more of that responsibility can sit with QuickEasy instead.

That means:

  • Less hardware for your business to buy and maintain
  • Less reliance on an on-site server
  • Less technical infrastructure for your team to worry about.

You still get the single system you need to run your entire business, but without having to manage as much of the technology that keeps them running.

Why run QuickEasy BOS in the cloud?

Easier access when your business operates in more than one place

Very few operational businesses now exist neatly inside one building. You may have an office and a factory. Stock may sit at another warehouse. Your financial manager may occasionally work from home. Management may travel between locations.

This is one of the clearest advantages of running QuickEasy in a hosted environment.

Your ERP does not have to be physically tied to one office server for authorised users to reach the hosted system. For a business with several locations, this can be far simpler than trying to make every branch depend on infrastructure sitting at head office.

There is no benefit in choosing cloud ERP simply because “cloud” sounds more modern. Choose it because the operating model suits your company better.

For many SMEs, QuickEasy in the cloud is the easier model because you keep the ERP capability while carrying less of the hosting burden yourself.

Cloud-based does not necessarily mean browser-based

This is one of the areas where ERP terminology becomes confusing. A system can be cloud-hosted without running inside Chrome, Edge or another web browser.

Cloud-based
Cloud-based describes where and how the ERP environment is hosted.
Browser-based
Browser-based describes the type of interface through which you use the application.

They are not interchangeable terms. This matters when you compare ERP products.

Don’t assume a product is more or less capable simply because it opens in a browser. Look at how the software performs, how users access it, how the hosting is managed, and whether the architecture suits the work your business actually does.

We deliver a native BOS environment through cloud infrastructure and virtualisation. This approach allows QuickEasy to retain the performance and functionality required by more complex ERP workloads.

For the buyer, the important point is straightforward: QuickEasy gives you a cloud ERP without limiting BOS to a browser-only experience.

Is QuickEasy cloud ERP secure?

Yes. QuickEasy BOS cloud-based ERP is designed to give your team easy access to the system while keeping your data safe, and limiting who can view, change, and approve important business information.

QuickEasy ERP’s key security controls include

Secure cloud access: Your team can access QuickEasy remotely through encrypted connections, helping protect your business data when people work from different locations.
User permissions: Give each person access only to the parts of the system they need. Your sales, accounts, production, and management teams do not all need to see the same information.
Approval controls: Set approval steps for important transactions such as quotes, purchase orders, and credit notes, so the right person signs off before they go ahead.
Audit trails: QuickEasy records system activity, including who made a change and when. This makes it easier to check what happened if there is a mistake, query, or dispute.
Role-specific views: Different users can have different dashboards, menus, and reports based on their role and level of authority.
Alerts and notifications: Managers can be notified when something needs attention, such as an approval, delay, or unusual activity.
Management reporting: High-level reports give decision-makers a clearer view of what is happening across the business, with the option to drill into the detail when needed.
Built-in business rules: Workflows, approval chains, and document processes can be set up around the way your business operates.

The important point is that security does not have to make the system harder to use. QuickEasy gives people the access they need to do their jobs while putting controls around sensitive information and important business decisions.

Who manages updates and upgrades with QuickEasy?

This is another important difference between buying old-style business software and subscribing to a modern ERP service. QuickEasy’s monthly BOS subscription includes access to upgrades, along with access to BOS modules and Helpdesk support.

That means you are not buying a fixed copy of ERP software and then simply leaving it unchanged for years.

We continually improve and develop BOS.

For a cloud customer, this sits alongside the wider benefit of having the hosting environment managed rather than owning the main ERP server yourself. The result is less technology for the business owner to personally coordinate.

The important thing is that you are not simply being handed new software and left to figure it out. We have decades of experience helping businesses move away from outdated systems, disconnected software, or no ERP system at all, and onto QuickEasy in a way that is practical and manageable.

FAQs

Can QuickEasy cloud ERP work across multiple locations?

A cloud-hosted environment is naturally suited to businesses where authorised users need to access BOS from different locations, provided those sites have suitable connectivity. QuickEasy’s technical specifications support its cloud-hosted deployment model across different user environments.

Can I move from locally hosted QuickEasy to the cloud?

Yes. QuickEasy supports both local and cloud hosting, so existing BOS customers should discuss migration options with QuickEasy. The exact process, data movement, hosting configuration and transition plan will depend on the existing setup.

What is the best cloud-based ERP for SMEs?

The best cloud ERP depends on the operation, not just company size. For growing manufacturing, printing, packaging, signage, engineering and similar operational businesses, QuickEasy BOS is a particularly strong option because it combines substantial ERP functionality with cloud hosting, published pricing, support and an on-premise option where required.

Why choose QuickEasy cloud ERP?

QuickEasy gives businesses the ability to run its complete BOS ERP in a hosted environment rather than maintaining the core ERP server entirely themselves. For an SME that wants serious operational ERP functionality with less infrastructure to manage on-premise, that combination makes QuickEasy one of the strongest cloud-based ERP choices available.
