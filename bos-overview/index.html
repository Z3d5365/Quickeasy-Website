BOS ERP

BOS ERP is business software that integrates back-office functions with front-office and manufacturing data. It takes care of the drudge work while removing human error.

BOS ERP is a central place for all your business data to be stored — silos are removed from departments that were previously working in isolation. It gives decision-makers access to important insights into your company in real time, identifying areas that could be hampering productivity or profitability, so business owners and managers have full insight across the business for accurate decision-making and improved efficiency.

Estimating & Quoting

Materials & Inventory

Multi-warehouse and multi-location management, with multiple suppliers and units linked to each item. Costs are adjusted with landing costs and the weighted average method; MRP and replenishment, batch/serial tracking and barcode scanning keep stock movements accurate.

Procurement

Manage multiple suppliers, units and currencies, with supplier price management and discount structures. MRP and replenishment optimise procurement against demand, backed by a robust requisition and approval process and accessible purchase history.

Production

Scheduling functionality, optionally based on Theory of Constraints (TOC), with job tracking and timekeeping for real-time monitoring via touch screens or scanners. Non-Conformance Reporting (NCR) flags quality issues, and job costing gives accurate cost analysis per job.

Tracking

Shop-floor job tracking, timekeeping and inventory movements via touch screen and scanners, plus mobile field service capabilities for travel logs, timekeeping, and images/signatures on the go — including digital projects and workflows for accountants, designers and architects.

Costing

Measure estimated versus actual quantities and costs, with robust reporting at item, transaction and customer level. Feedback loops improve estimating models and enhance cost management and decision-making.

Accounting

Ensures compliance with GAAP and IFRS, supporting multiple entities with separate financial reporting, multiple currencies, budgeting, bank imports and management reporting. Batch processing covers subscriptions, contract billing and supplier payments, with easy error resolution.

Management

Construction

While resource-based platforms typically share common characteristics, construction ERP specifically requires certain discrete operational capabilities. Large-scale construction development usually involves not only transaction-based processing but also detailed cost estimation ahead of the bid-contract-sale chain — at a systems level, this drives a number of highly granular business needs. Consequently, BOS Enterprise creates maximum business value.

Food and Beverage

A good ERP system enables a food company to know when it is low on a particular inventory item so it can be refilled immediately, and to allow for fluctuation during peak and dip product seasons. Ordering just enough inventory meets demand while keeping waste to a minimum — profitable for any business, but especially one where expiration dates force a product to move quickly. BOS ERP also has track-and-trace functionality so you're always compliant with regulations.
