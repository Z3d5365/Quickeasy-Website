BOS ERP

Complete business management and ERP software.

BOS ERP is business software that integrates back-office functions with front-office and manufacturing data. It takes care of the drudge work while removing human error.

BOS ERP is a central place for all your business data to be stored. Silos are removed from departments that were previously working in isolation.

BOS ERP gives decision-makers access to important insights into your company in real time. It identifies areas that could be hampering productivity or profitability.

BOS ERP allows you to streamline daily operations and makes it easier to run your business. Now, business owners and managers have insight across the entire business for accurate decision-making and improved efficiency.

Service Manager & Sales

The Service Manager module provides end-to-end management of the customer engagement lifecycle. From initial sales prospecting to opportunity closure. It uses intuitive dashboards and reports. It also streamlines customer service processes across departments. This enables efficient handling of sales, production, and accounting enquiries, as well as contracted services like maintenance, repairs, and metered offerings. Discover Service Manager

Project Management

The Project Management tool provides a structured way to cost, plan, execute, and track projects across your organisation. From creating project templates and assigning tasks to managing timelines and budgets, this functionality ensures full visibility and control. Integrated checklists, milestones, and progress tracking help teams stay aligned and deliver on time — every time. Discover Project Management

Estimating & Quoting

Fixed estimating models can be tailored for industries such as Print, Packaging, Signage, and more. These models include comprehensive costings to support accurate estimates. Custom estimating models can be configured to fill the gaps. Users can adjust quotations using customer price lists and generate customised quote letters and proposals as desired. Discover Estimating

Materials & Inventory

The materials and inventory modules support multi-warehouse and multi-location management. Multiple suppliers and units can be linked to each item. Inventory costs are adjusted with landing costs and updated using the weighted average costing method. MRP and replenishment capabilities are provided. Stock can be easily tracked by batch or serial number. Barcode scanning is available to efficiently manage stock movements. Discover Materials & Inventory

Procurement

The procurement functionality supports the management of multiple suppliers, units, and currencies. Supplier price management capabilities and discount structures are available. With MRP and replenishment functionalities, businesses can optimise procurement based on demand and inventory levels. The module also encompasses a robust requisition and approval process and the ability to track outstanding orders. Additionally, the module provides readily accessible purchase history information. Discover Procurement

Production

The production modules include scheduling functionality, optionally based on the Theory of Constraints (TOC) methodology. Job tracking and timekeeping capabilities enable real-time monitoring and tracking of production progress using touch screens or scanners. Non-Conformance Reporting (NCR) functionality enables the identification and reporting of quality issues or deviations from specifications during production. Job costing capabilities provide accurate cost analysis and tracking for each production job. Discover Production Management

Tracking

The tracker module includes shop-floor job tracking, timekeeping, and inventory movements. This is done using touch screen and scanners. Functionality also encompasses mobile field service capabilities, facilitating travel logs, timekeeping, and capturing images and signatures on the go. Additionally, users are able to manage digital projects and workflows. This caters to professionals such as accountants, designers, and architects. Discover Tracking

Costing

The costing analysis functionality encompasses the ability to measure estimated versus actual quantities and costs. This offers robust reporting features at multiple levels, such as item, transaction, and customer. Moreover, the module allows for valuable feedback to improve estimating models and enhance cost management and decision-making. Discover Costing

Accounting

The accounting functionality ensures compliance with GAAP and IFRS. The functionality supports multiple entities, allowing for separate financial reporting. It also accommodates multiple Currencies, Budgeting, Bank imports and Management Reporting capabilities. Batch processing features are available for various functions such as subscriptions, contract billing, and supplier payments. Easily find and resolve accounting errors. Discover Accounting

Reporting

Reporting functionality includes interactive fixed reporting for key modules. Such as Sales, Inventory, Accounting, and Cost of Sales, offering drill-down functionality to delve into detailed data. Functionality enables the creation of custom reports tailored to specific business needs. Additionally, users have the option to receive automated notifications related to specific events

Dashboards

The dashboards functionality offers a wide range of integrated dashboards and summaries. It provides a consolidated view of key performance indicators (KPIs) related to sales, orders, inventory, production, and accounting. The dashboards provide intuitive visualisations and data summaries. This enables users to quickly identify trends, make informed decisions, and drive business growth.

Management

Management functionality encompasses User Access Control for administrators to manage permissions and access rights. It also includes Audits to track and review system activities, hierarchical Approvals, and availability of Executive Summaries. Notifications, Reports, and Dashboards for informed decision-making. Discover Management

Construction

While resources-based platforms typically encompass common characteristics, when it comes to construction ERP specifically, certain discrete operational requirements must apply as well.

Because large-scale construction development usually involves itself with not only transaction-based processing but also the projection of detailed cost estimations prior to the efficient execution and delivery of the bid-contract-sale chain, at a systems level, this focus tends to drive a number of different and highly granular business needs.

Consequently, BOS Enterprise creates maximum business value.

Food and Beverage

A good ERP system, enables a food company to know when it is low on a particular inventory item so that it can be refilled immediately. It also allows for fluctuation during peak and dip product seasons. By ordering just enough inventory, demand is met and inventory waste is kept to a minimum. Responding in this as-needed fashion is profitable for any business but especially one in which expiration dates force a product to move quickly.

BOS ERP also has track and trace functionality so that you are always compliant to regulations.
