BOS Management Features and Benefits

Our Management tools empower leadership and administrators with the tools to oversee operations, enforce controls, and make data-driven decisions. From user access and audit trails to approvals and executive dashboards, it ensures your business remains secure, compliant and aligned with strategic goals.

Discover how this cutting-edge solution can transform your organisation.

General

Management, control and oversight — built in.

User Access Control

Protect your data and processes by assigning role-based permissions, ensuring users only access the functions and information relevant to their responsibilities.

Hierarchical Approvals

Enforce accountability through structured approval workflows for quotes, purchases, credits and more, aligned with your organisation's chain of command.

Audit Logs

Track system activity with detailed records of who did what, when, and where — supporting compliance, troubleshooting, and accountability.

Executive Summaries

Get a snapshot of performance with high-level views of key business indicators across departments to support informed, timely decision-making.

Custom Notifications

Stay informed in real-time with automated alerts for key events like approvals, status changes, delays, or anomalies.

Interactive Reports

Dive deeper into operational data with filtering, sorting, and drilldown capabilities to uncover actionable insights.

Role-Specific Views

Deliver the right information to the right people by tailoring modules, menus, dashboards and reports by role, department, or level of authority.

System Configuration

Align the platform with your business rules through flexible settings that govern workflow behaviour, document templates, and approval chains.
