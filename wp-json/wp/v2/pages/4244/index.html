1.\u00a0Introduction<\/strong><\/h4>\n

QuickEasy Software is dedicated to safeguarding your personal and business data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect the information you enter into the BOS ERP Application.<\/p>\n

2.\u00a0Information We Collect<\/strong><\/h4>\n

All data entered into the BOS ERP Application by users, including:<\/p>\n

    \n
  • Personal Data<\/strong>: Usernames, contact information, and other personal details.<\/li>\n
  • Business Data<\/strong>: Sensitive business information such as customer records, supplier details, financial and commercial data.<\/li>\n<\/ul>\n

    3.\u00a0Data Storage and Exclusivity<\/strong><\/h4>\n

    All data you enter is stored within the BOS ERP Application and remains within that system exclusively. Each BOS ERP Application instance is dedicated solely to the customer who owns it, ensuring that your data is not shared across systems.<\/p>\n

    4.\u00a0How We Use Your Information<\/strong><\/h4>\n

    Your data is used to:<\/p>\n

      \n
    • Provide and enhance the BOS ERP services.<\/li>\n
    • Perform necessary maintenance and updates.<\/li>\n
    • Offer support and troubleshoot issues.<\/li>\n<\/ul>\n

      When We Access Your Data:<\/p>\n

        \n
      • QuickEasy Software will only access your data when necessary for technical support or maintenance, and only with your permission.<\/li>\n<\/ul>\n

        5.\u00a0Data Confidentiality<\/strong><\/h4>\n

        We understand the sensitivity of your business data. All information entered into the BOS ERP Application will be kept strictly confidential.<\/p>\n

        6.\u00a0Data Security<\/strong><\/h4>\n

        We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data from unauthorised access, use, or disclosure. This includes encryption, access controls, and regular security audits.<\/p>\n

        7.\u00a0Data Sharing<\/strong><\/h4>\n

        We do not share your business or personal data with third parties except:<\/p>\n

          \n
        • Service Providers<\/strong>: When essential for system operations and maintenance.<\/li>\n
        • Legal Compliance<\/strong>: If required by law.<\/li>\n<\/ul>\n

          8.\u00a0User Rights<\/strong><\/h4>\n

          As the user, you have full control over your data within the BOS ERP Application. You can directly access, review, correct, delete, or manage your data at any time through the application’s built-in tools and features. You also have the ability to control how and when your data is shared.<\/p>\n

          9.\u00a0Data Backup and Recovery<\/strong><\/h4>\n

          When customers use AWS through QuickEasy, with QuickEasy managing the customer’s AWS instance, we regularly back up the data stored in the BOS ERP Application to ensure its safety and availability. These backups are governed by the same privacy policy and access restrictions as the live data, ensuring that your information remains secure and confidential even in backup form.<\/p>\n

          10.\u00a0User Consent<\/strong><\/h4>\n

          By using the BOS ERP Application, you consent to the collection, use, and protection of your data as described in this Privacy Policy.<\/p>\n

          11.\u00a0Changes to This Policy<\/strong><\/h4>\n

          We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be communicated through the application or via email.<\/p>\n
