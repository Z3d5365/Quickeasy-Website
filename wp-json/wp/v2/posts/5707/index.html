    The Surprising Impact of Spreadsheets<\/h2>\n\n\n\n

    Spreadsheets are like that old bakkie you keep promising yourself you\u2019ll replace: dependable, familiar, but increasingly a liability and – if you dare admit it – too slow for your needs. <\/p>\n\n\n\n

    Spreadsheets give you a false sense of control. But they quietly lock you into an endless cycle of manual admin, tabs, and \u201cversion control\u201d that lives somewhere between your laptop, your assistant\u2019s inbox, and that mysterious \u201cfinal-final-FINAL.xlsx\u201d file.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

    The result? A permanent sense of dread that if you step away for too long, the business will collapse.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

    What starts as \u201cquick and simple\u201d becomes an admin trap. A gilded cage. Beautifully coloured cells, fancy formulas, and conditional formatting that\u2019s practically art, but a cage nonetheless.<\/p>\n\n\n\n
