Production adds instructions manually. Stores checks stock separately. Customer service asks for updates. Accounts waits for delivery confirmation before invoicing. The more manual handover points you have, the easier it is for details to slip.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

      QuickEasy BOS ERP connects approved estimates to jobs, work orders, job cards, routing forms, production stages and work in progress<\/strong>.         4. Job costing shows what the job really cost<\/h3>\n\n\n\n

        A job can be accepted at the right price and still lose margin during production. That can happen because material usage was higher than expected, wastage was too high, labour ran over, machine time was underestimated, rework was needed, finishing took longer, outsourcing was more expensive, or delivery costs were not properly allowed for. This is why job costing is one of the most important capabilities in printing industry software.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

        QuickEasy BOS ERP connect job costing information across estimating, stock, production, purchasing and accounting.         5. WIP tracking shows where every job stands<\/h3>\n\n\n\n

        Work in progress is one of the biggest visibility gaps in many print businesses. A job may be waiting for artwork. Another is waiting for approval. One is in production. One is at finishing. One is packed but not dispatched. One has been delivered but not invoiced. Another has a problem, but sales does not know yet.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

        If WIP is tracked through paper job cards, whiteboards, spreadsheets or verbal updates, your team has to keep asking each other what is happening.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

        QuickEasy BOS ERP helps track work through the business so teams can see job status more clearly.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

        Sales can check where a customer\u2019s job is. Production can see what needs to move next. Stores can see what materials are required. Customer service can give better updates. Accounts can see when a job is ready to invoice. Management can see delays, bottlenecks and jobs at risk. This is particularly important in print, signage and packaging because jobs often move through several departments before completion. Better WIP tracking gives the business one clearer view of the work in motion. It helps reduce chasing, missed handovers, late surprises and jobs that quietly sit somewhere in the system without enough attention.<\/p>\n\n\n\n
