When Standalone Tools Cause Project Delays, or Worse, Failure<\/h2>\n\n\n\n

If you\u2019re using separate tools for project planning, task allocation, time tracking, stock control, and invoicing, you are likely facing the same frustrations many of our clients once did.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

  • Redundant data entry<\/strong>: Manual and redundant data capture takes time. And time is money. Not only is it extra work to capture information multiple times across systems, it exposes your team and your project to risk from human error.<\/li>\n\n\n\n
  • Gaps in visibility<\/strong>: You can\u2019t see, in real time, how project progress is affected by – and affects – people resources, stock levels, costing, or timelines. If you can\u2019t see it, you can\u2019t manage it.<\/li>\n\n\n\n
  • Delayed decision-making<\/strong>: Waiting on reports from various departments often means you react to issues too late.<\/li>\n\n\n\n
  • No single source of truth<\/strong>: When systems don\u2019t speak to each other, no one\u2019s looking at the same data, or the same version of that data.<\/li>\n\n\n\n
  • Overspending and under delivering<\/strong>: When there are so many moving parts, and so little clarity, it follows that budgets are blown and deadlines are theoretical.<\/li>\n<\/ul>\n\n\n\n
