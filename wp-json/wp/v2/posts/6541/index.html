The time comes in most business owner\u2019s journeys when the need to move from outdated or paper-based systems becomes business-critical<\/strong>. And it’s a big undertaking. Once you\u2019ve unearthed how much an ERP system costs, once you\u2019ve decided which ERP system to use, and if you want a cloud-based ERP<\/strong><\/a> or a local server, you are now faced with the ERP data migration.\u00a0<\/p>\n\n\n\n

A good ERP data migration starts by deciding what your business actually needs. Current customers, suppliers, stock information, materials, balances, pricing, and operational data may need to move. Duplicate or obsolete records should usually be cleaned up first. Some older history may be better kept as an archive. <\/p>\n\n\n\n

The aim is not to recreate your old ERP inside the new one. It is to start with accurate, useful information that your team can trust. When we migrate a business onto QuickEasy BOS, data conversion is one part of a wider implementation process that includes configuration, testing, user sign-off, cut-over, balancing, training, and go-live support.<\/p>\n\n\n\n
