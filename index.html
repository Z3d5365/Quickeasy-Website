Complete business management & ERP software

Run your whole business on one system.

QuickEasy BOS is an all-in-one, cloud-based ERP that brings your finances, operations, sales and people into a single application — fully managed and supported, at a fraction of the cost of legacy ERP suites.

Sound familiar?

Your business has outgrown the way it runs.

Most growing companies end up in one of these three situations — and each one quietly costs time, money and accuracy every day.

01

Accounting app + a wall of spreadsheets

Your real operational data lives in spreadsheets bolted onto your accounting package. Every report becomes a monthly reconciliation exercise instead of a decision.

02

A pile of apps that don’t talk

A different tool for every job, none of them sharing data. The same information gets captured twice — and still drifts out of sync between systems.

03

An old, clunky legacy ERP

An on-premise system that’s slow to change, expensive to maintain, and needs specialist IT just to keep running — and nobody enjoys using it.

One system. Everything connected.

QuickEasy BOS brings it all into one place.

Not another app to integrate — a single application where finance and operations already share the same data.

One source of truth

A quote, a job, your stock and the ledger all reflect the same numbers in real time — no more spreadsheets kept alongside your accounts.

Natively integrated, not bolted together

Every function is part of the same system. No exports, no double capture, and no fragile links between point apps that break when you least expect it.

Modern cloud, always current

Access it securely from anywhere, on any device, with no servers to maintain — updates and hosting are handled for you.
