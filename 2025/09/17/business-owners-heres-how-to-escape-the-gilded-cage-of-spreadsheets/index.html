Business Owners, Here’s How to Escape the Gilded Cage of Spreadsheets

17 September 2025

You probably didn’t leave your cushy corporate job for the joys of working longer hours. The dream was freedom, wasn’t it? More time for the kids’ cricket matches, the occasional weekday round of golf, and maybe even a holiday that doesn’t involve answering emails in the hotel’s Wi-Fi zone.

But here’s the rub: instead of freedom, many South African business owners find themselves shackled. Not by a ball and chain, but by something far less shiny yet deceptively innocent… spreadsheets.

Key takeaways:

  • Spreadsheets and outdated tech hamstring your business, and your freedom.
  • A single system approach stops business owners from being the bottleneck, and improves efficiency.
  • What does this really mean? You get your evenings back, you can go on holiday, your business runs efficiently, even if you’re not there.

The Surprising Impact of Spreadsheets

Spreadsheets are like that old bakkie you keep promising yourself you’ll replace: dependable, familiar, but increasingly a liability and – if you dare admit it – too slow for your needs.

Spreadsheets give you a false sense of control. But they quietly lock you into an endless cycle of manual admin, tabs, and “version control” that lives somewhere between your laptop, your assistant’s inbox, and that mysterious “final-final-FINAL.xlsx” file.

The result? A permanent sense of dread that if you step away for too long, the business will collapse.

What starts as “quick and simple” becomes an admin trap. A gilded cage. Beautifully coloured cells, fancy formulas, and conditional formatting that’s practically art, but a cage nonetheless.

In fact, a survey of 2,000 employees in the US and UK revealed that – while there’s nothing inherently wrong with spreadsheets – they are simply not built to run a business.

Here are five common problems businesses face when spreadsheets are their backbone.

ProblemWhat it looks like in your businessWhy it hurts
1. Loss of Historical DataYou archive old files, rename them “v1”, “v2,” etc., or move them around so much nobody remembers where last year’s stock numbers are.Without reliable history you can’t make accurate decisions. Guesswork replaces strategy.
2. Version Control ChaosMultiple staff have their own “final” version. You’re never 100% sure which is “the” report.Mistakes creep in. Wrong decisions get made.
3. Growth and Performance ProblemsAs data volumes grow, the spreadsheet slows down; formulas break; opening files on your phone becomes impossible; sharing becomes a nightmare.You lose agility. What used to be a quick check becomes a half-hour ordeal.
4. Lack of Documentation and StandardisationSomeone leaves, and critical knowledge walks out the door.This means errors multiply, onboarding new staff becomes painful, and audits or compliance are riskier because data trails are messy or missing.
5. Manual Admin = Human ErrorDuplicate work, copy-pasting, merging sheets, manually collating data from different departments or branches. Every time a human touches the data, there’s room for slip-ups.Missed opportunities, overstock or understock, mispriced quotes, all these cost real money and damage reputation. Also burns up staff time better spent on profitable work.
