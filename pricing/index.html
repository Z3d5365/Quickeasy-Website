Why invest in BOS?

Tangible returns through increased sales and cost savings

Investing in QuickEasy Software's ERP solutions offers tangible returns for businesses through increased sales and cost savings.

  • Sales Activity Recording — retains crucial sales data even after a salesperson departs, ensuring continuity and leverage of past efforts.
  • Quick Estimating — accelerates the quoting process, making your business more responsive and competitive.
  • Flexible Quoting — offers the ability to present alternative quotes with more cost-effective sizes or materials, increasing the likelihood of closing sales.
  • Better Negotiation — accurate cost insights empower stronger negotiation positions.
  • Quotation Follow-ups — integrated tools ensure timely engagement with potential customers, boosting conversion rates.
  • Process Efficiency — eliminates duplicate processes, reducing the need for redundant staffing and streamlining workflow from quote to final transaction.
  • Smarter Purchasing — facilitates informed purchasing decisions with detailed purchase histories and supplier price comparisons, leading to cost reductions.
  • Optimized Production — improves planning for production, resulting in lower overtime and downtime.
  • Reduced Human Error — minimizes mistakes by ensuring consistency between estimated and ordered materials, enhancing overall operational accuracy.
