Apply to become a Referral Partner

QuickEasy is a leading software company developing and providing Management Information Systems and ERP systems to businesses globally. Our Referral Partner Program is designed for individuals and companies looking to refer new business leads, where you can earn a monthly fee based on the monthly subscription fees of any customers you refer to us. This program is open to:

  • Individuals — you'll receive direct payment for successful referrals.
  • Customer Companies — referral credits are applied to your monthly fees.
  • Non-Customer Companies — payments are made quarterly for eligible referrals.

Why should I become a QuickEasy Referral Partner?

We're big on easy — from CEO to newest recruit, we believe in keeping things simple and easy to work with.

You will receive an ongoing percentage of the monthly subscription fees for each successful referral, either as direct payments or as referral credits if you're a current customer.

There are no long-term obligations and no need for a formal contract — enjoy a streamlined process with support from the QuickEasy team and acknowledgement for your contributions.

Important information you need to know

We're looking for individuals and companies that have strong industry connections and a solid understanding of business processes. Ideal Referral Partners should possess the following qualities:

  • Strong Business Acumen — ability to identify and understand business needs and recognise potential fits for QuickEasy's solutions.
  • Effective Communication — confidence in discussing the benefits of QuickEasy and sharing success stories.
  • Professional Conduct — maintains a high level of professionalism during all interactions.
  • Credibility and Trustworthiness — seen as a reliable source of information and guidance by their peers and network.
  • Industry Knowledge — familiarity with ERP software and business management processes is an advantage.
